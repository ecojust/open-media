# Background music notices

The ten bundled instrumental selections are distributed under CC0 1.0 Universal. Each original author's publication and license declaration is preserved in `sources/`; the complete legal text is in `CC0-1.0.txt`. The source URLs, original and distributed file sizes and SHA-256 hashes, original titles, creator credits, and exact excerpt/encoding changes are recorded in `manifest.json`.

The Chinese display titles describe the curated selections; they do not replace or claim ownership of the original works. These are distinct existing instrumental compositions, not generated voice samples. CC0 permits copying, adaptation, redistribution, and use in exported projects without a mandatory attribution requirement. Creator credits are retained here voluntarily.

The audio files are 44.1 kHz stereo MP3 at 160 kbps, normalized to a target of -18 LUFS. Longer tracks are offered as 120-second excerpts with a three-second fade-out; shorter works retain their full musical duration. Exact processing is recorded per track.
